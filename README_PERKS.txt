Until Zombies Come Online v3.1 PERKS

New perks (shop):
- Extra Game! EUR 1000: 2x magazine size for every weapon.
- Bigg Bos EUR 1500: 2x weapon damage (including explosive pistol).
- Illusionist EUR 1500: every 3 seconds a bomb targets a strong nearby zombie in your current zone, dealing splash damage.

Each perk is bought once per match, for the player who purchased it.

Deploy:
1. Upload server.js to the repository root, replacing the existing server.js.
2. Upload public/index.html to the public folder, replacing the existing index.html.
3. Commit changes, wait for Render deployment to become Live, refresh with Ctrl+F5.

Keep the previous version as backup. Online gameplay has not been verified with two actual players.
